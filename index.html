Rules

  • No target goes below 1,700 kcal.
  • A day's target can rise at most 250 kcal above its plan, so a light day isn't banked and eaten back later.
  • Days you haven't logged in the past count as eaten at target.
  • Judge the 7-day average weight only, weighed each morning after the toilet and before eating.
  • Losing more than 0.5 kg a week on the average: add 150 kcal a day.
  • No movement after 3 weeks: check logging accuracy first (oils, wine, shared dishes), then cut 100 to 150.
  • Christmas events: eat them, and don't compensate before or after.
